# Hiasynth

Hiasynth answers questions about people, places and markets in Europe from a 1:1 synthetic population (522 million people, ~300 attributes, down to municipality and 1 km²). Use it for any question about how many people there are, who they are, what they earn, own or spend, or where they live — give every call a place, a country at minimum. Start with `analyze_market` for sizing, `describe_area` for "who lives in X", and `reach_audience` for channels. Money columns ending in `_tlc` are thousands of local currency and must not be compared across countries.
